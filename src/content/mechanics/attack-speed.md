# Attack Speed & Turn Order

In **Idle Guild Master**, combat rounds are determined by adventurers' and enemies' **Attack Speed**.

## Mechanics Overview

* Each combat participant accumulates initiative each tick proportional to their **Attack Speed**.
* When a unit reaches 100% action bar, they take their action (Standard Strike, Active Skill, or Potion Drink).
* Items providing `Attack Speed +X` directly accelerate action gauge accumulation.
