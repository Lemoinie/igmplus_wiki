# Basic Atk Amp

In **Idle Guild Master Modded (IGM+)**, **Basic Atk Amp** (formerly *Normal Atk Amp*) is the dedicated scaling multiplier for standard attacks.

## Core Rules

* **Baseline:** All entities start at a baseline of **100% (1.0×)** Basic Atk Amp.
* **Scope:** Exclusively scales:
  * Standard weapon attacks performed during normal attack rounds.
  * Extra strikes generated by **Attack Speed**.
  * Basic single-target round-based healing.
* **Separation from Skills:** Does **not** modify active skill attacks, spells, or active skill heals (which are governed by Skill Amp).
* **Inspection Dialog:** Inspectable on Page 3 of the hero and enemy detail dialogs.

## Formula

**Basic Strike Damage** = **Weapon Damage** × **Damage Dealt Modifier** × **Basic Attack Amp**

## Synergies & Trade-Offs

* **First Strike Amplification (Alert+):** Evolving Alert via the Evo-24 Vial into **Alert+** grants **+20% Basic Atk Amp** on the very first hit delivered in each combat encounter.
* **Deadly Finesse Archetype:** High-attack-speed rogue specializations intentionally trade Basic Atk Amp for extra hits:
  * **Deadly Finesse I (Meat Carver):** +50% Attack Speed, -25% Basic Atk Amp
  * **Deadly Finesse II (Wounds Weaver):** +75% Attack Speed, -35% Basic Atk Amp
  * **Deadly Finesse III (Hellish Sculptor & Spirit Engraver):** +100% Attack Speed, -50% Basic Atk Amp
  * **Status Stacking Benefit:** Reduced basic hit damage is heavily offset by the sheer volume of attacks applying full-value status effects (e.g. Bleed stacks shredding enemy armor) while active skills remain at full potency.
