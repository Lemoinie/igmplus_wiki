# Damage Dealt

In **Idle Guild Master Modded (IGM+)**, **Damage Dealt** is the universal multiplier that scales all outgoing damage produced by an entity.

## Core Rules

* **Baseline:** All adventurers and enemies operate at a baseline of **100% (1.0×)** Damage Dealt.
* **Universal Scaling:** Unlike Basic Atk Amp or Skill Amp, Damage Dealt multiplies **all outgoing damage**—including standard weapon strikes, extra attacks from Attack Speed, and active skill bursts—before target defense is calculated.
* **Inspection Dialog:** Viewable on Page 3 of the hero and enemy inspection dialogs.

## Multiplier Formula

**Final Outgoing Damage** = **Base Damage** × **Damage Dealt Modifier**

## Modifiers & Status Effects

* **Equipment & Gear:** Weapons, armor sets, and boss accessories can grant direct percent increases to Damage Dealt.
* **Positive Combat Buffs:**
  * **Delirium:** +100% Damage Dealt
  * **Skeleton Key:** +100% Damage Dealt
  * **Frenzy:** +30% Damage Dealt
  * **Anointed / Inspire / Exalt:** +25% Damage Dealt
  * **Sanguine Fervor:** +5% Damage Dealt per remaining stack
* **Negative Debuffs:**
  * **Entangle:** -20% Damage Dealt
* **Doctrines:**
  * **Ragebound (Doctrine of Ruin):** Increases both Damage Dealt and Damage Taken.
