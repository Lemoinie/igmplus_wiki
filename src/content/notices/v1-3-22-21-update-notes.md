### 1. Flexible Status Immunity & Status Resistance Systems:

<!-- rb:block {"type":"bullets","items":[{"text":"**Percentage\u002dBased Specific Status Immunity (0%–100%):** Specific status immunities on entities and equipment now use percentage chances rather than binary 100% flags, rolling independently from General Status Immunity when a status is applied.","level":0},{"text":"**General & Specific Status Resistance (0%–100%):** Reduces incoming DoT damage (Ablaze, Hellfire, Bloodflame, Bleed, Frozen, Entangle, Terrify) and Thorns recoil damage after attacker bonuses, reducing damage to 0 at 100% resistance.","level":0},{"text":"**Turn\u002dStart CC Self\u002dCleanse:** Units afflicted by cleansable CC (Stun, Petrify, Silence, Terrify, Confuse) roll General and Specific Status Resistance separately at the start of their turn to break free and act normally (Terrify deals its reduced DoT tick before rolling to cleanse; uncleansable stuns and soft CCs Entangle/Frozen do not self\u002dcleanse).","level":0}]} -->
* **Percentage-Based Specific Status Immunity (0%–100%):** Specific status immunities on entities and equipment now use percentage chances rather than binary 100% flags, rolling independently from General Status Immunity when a status is applied.
* **General & Specific Status Resistance (0%–100%):** Reduces incoming DoT damage (Ablaze, Hellfire, Bloodflame, Bleed, Frozen, Entangle, Terrify) and Thorns recoil damage after attacker bonuses, reducing damage to 0 at 100% resistance.
* **Turn-Start CC Self-Cleanse:** Units afflicted by cleansable CC (Stun, Petrify, Silence, Terrify, Confuse) roll General and Specific Status Resistance separately at the start of their turn to break free and act normally (Terrify deals its reduced DoT tick before rolling to cleanse; uncleansable stuns and soft CCs Entangle/Frozen do not self-cleanse).

<!-- rb:block {"type":"heading","level":3,"text":"2. Allied Battlefield Cap (15 Units) & Summon Logic:"} -->
### 2. Allied Battlefield Cap (15 Units) & Summon Logic:

<!-- rb:block {"type":"bullets","items":[{"text":"Enforced a strict 15\u002dunit maximum on allied combatants to match the 15 unit frames in the dungeon UI and eliminate off\u002dscreen \"ghost\" units.","level":0},{"text":"Necromancer / Lich: Blocked spawn minion past 15 units; casting Curse when the battlefield is full now heals active bound minions instead of failing.","level":0},{"text":"Beast Tamer & Logging: Deduplicated capacity log messages so Beast Tamers do not spam warnings every turn; when any ally or summon dies, the freed slot immediately resets warning flags.","level":0}]} -->
* Enforced a strict 15-unit maximum on allied combatants to match the 15 unit frames in the dungeon UI and eliminate off-screen "ghost" units.
* Necromancer / Lich: Blocked spawn minion past 15 units; casting Curse when the battlefield is full now heals active bound minions instead of failing.
* Beast Tamer & Logging: Deduplicated capacity log messages so Beast Tamers do not spam warnings every turn; when any ally or summon dies, the freed slot immediately resets warning flags.
