Welcome to the brand new **Notice & News Hub** for Idle Guild Master Modded!

### Why a Dedicated Notice Page?

Discord is great for quick conversations, but its **2,000 character limit** makes long-form game updates, in-depth patch notes, and mathematical explanations nearly impossible to format properly. 

Whenever we published large balance updates, we were forced to:
* Chop changelogs into 5+ fragmented messages.
* Sacrifice readable comparison tables.
* Omit high-resolution sprites and screenshot comparisons.

### What You Can Expect Here

1. **Unrestricted Length**: Hundred-line patch notes, developer diaries, and design retrospectives without truncation.
2. **Interactive Comparison Tables**: Side-by-side numerical diffs for stats, formulas, damage percentages, and drop rates.
3. **Rich Media**: High-definition pixel-art sprites, area banners, and before/after comparisons.
4. **Seamless Discord Sharing**: Every notice has a dedicated URL with OpenGraph metadata. When you share a notice link in Discord, it automatically produces a rich visual card with title, banner, and summary! Use the **Copy Discord Link** button at the top of any notice to grab it with one tap.

Feel free to browse our archives or filter by category above!
